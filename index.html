Privacy Policy — Last updated: October 10, 2026

Overview

LockVibe AI is an AI-powered lock screen wallpaper generator. This privacy policy explains what data we collect and how we use it. Our core principle: no account, no backend, no tracking.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account required. LockVibe AI does not ask you to sign up or log in.
  • No personal data. We do not collect your name, email, location, or device identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking. We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, ad networks, or tracking frameworks.
  • No backend server. The app runs entirely on your device. There is no server storing your data.

What Happens When You Generate a Wallpaper

When you tap "Generate," your device sends a text description (the style and color palette you selected) to our AI image generation provider, fal.ai, which returns a generated image. Here is what is sent and what is not:

  • Sent to fal.ai: A short text prompt describing the wallpaper style and colors (e.g., "minimalist, blue tones"). No personal data is included.
  • Not sent: Your photos, contacts, location, or any other personal information.
  • Stored on device: The generated wallpaper image is saved locally on your iPhone. It is also saved to your Photos library only when you explicitly tap "Save."

fal.ai's own privacy practices are governed by their privacy policy. We recommend reviewing it.

Photos Access

LockVibe AI requests access to your Photos library only to save wallpapers you create so you can set them as your lock screen. We use the "Add Photos Only" permission — we can save images to your library but cannot read or access your existing photos. We never upload, share, or transmit your photos.

In-App Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple's StoreKit framework. We do not see, store, or process your payment information. All billing is handled by Apple under your Apple ID.

Data Retention

  • On your device: Your generated wallpapers and favorites are stored in the app's local database. Deleting the app removes all of this data.
  • On fal.ai servers: Generated images may be temporarily cached by fal.ai during processing. We do not control their retention — see their privacy policy for details.

Children's Privacy

LockVibe AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete on our servers. All your data lives on your device and is under your control. Deleting the app erases everything.

Contact Us
